--- reading ---
The lemma is an upper bound on bridge curvature that holds for EVERY
positive edge-length assignment, so it also holds along the entire
flow trajectory. A topology-preserving flow can never delete the
bridge, so the constraint is permanent. This is the analytic reason
surgery is required: Ma-Yang delete exactly these edges.

Caveat: the lemma bounds ONE edge. Turning it into a variance lower
bound needs a matching lower bound on non-bridge curvature, which we
do not have. State the lemma as an obstruction, not as a full bound
on the residual.
