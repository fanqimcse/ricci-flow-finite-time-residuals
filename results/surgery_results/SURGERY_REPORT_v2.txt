  ANSWER B. Where surgery actually removes a meaningful number of
  edges and the arm runs to completion, the residual collapses.
  The plateau is largely the cost of NOT doing surgery.

  Consequence for the paper: the claim cannot be 'a structural
  obstruction to Ricci flow'. It must be 'the uniformisation limit
  of the TOPOLOGY-PRESERVING flow' - how far a graph is from
  constant curvature if its edge set may not change. Surgery-based
  flows (Ma-Yang 2025) destroy exactly this quantity, so the result
  is complementary to, not in conflict with, that literature.
